## 1. University Overview

**Q: What is Sreenidhi University?**
Sreenidhi University is a premier private university recognised under the Telangana State Private Universities Act of 2018, approved by the Government of Telangana vide G.O.Ms. No.55 dated 30th July, 2024. It builds on the legacy of the Sreenidhi Education Group as a multidisciplinary, future-facing university. Its current programmes are in engineering — B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering and its specialisations — and in business, through the BBA programmes, with a long-term vision of expanding into further future-focused domains over time. The university combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications and is positioned as a future-facing institution focused on long-term professional adaptability.

**Q: Which programmes does Sreenidhi University offer?**
Sreenidhi University currently offers two undergraduate streams. The Sreenidhi School of Engineering runs five B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering tracks: CSE (Core); CSE (AI & ML); CSE (Data Science); CSE (Cyber Security); and CSE (AI & SAP Cloud), also known as Enterprise AI with SAP Cloud. The Sreenidhi School of Business runs the BBA programmes: BBA (General & Honours); FinTech; Business Intelligence & AI; Marketing Management; Innovation & Entrepreneurship; and Supply Chain Management. These B.Tech CSE and BBA programmes are the complete set of degrees offered for the current intake, with a long-term vision of expanding into further future-focused domains over time.

**Q: Do you offer MBBS, law, B.Com, M.Tech, Mechanical, Civil, or other programmes?**
Not in the current intake. For now, Sreenidhi University's degrees are the B.Tech CSE tracks and the BBA programmes listed above — so courses such as MBBS, law, B.Com, M.Tech, standalone Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and other programmes aren't offered this year. As a future-facing university we do expect to broaden into more domains over time, so a programme like that may open in a future year, though I can't promise a specific course or date yet. In the meantime I'd love to help you find the closest fit among what we run today: if you enjoy building software and systems, B.Tech CSE (Core) is a natural starting point; for AI, analytics or data, CSE (AI & ML) or CSE (Data Science); for security, CSE (Cyber Security); for enterprise and ERP, CSE (AI & SAP Cloud); and if your interest is business, finance or management, the BBA programmes — including FinTech and Business Intelligence & AI — are a strong route. One clarification on engineering: Mechanical and Civil are not separate B.Tech degrees here — they appear only as a few open-elective courses within the B.Tech programme, not as a branch you can graduate in.

**Q: Do BBA students learn AI and data skills?**
Yes — all BBA students are introduced to AI in Business, Python for Managers, and Analytics in the first year, making them digitally fluent and industry-relevant from the start.

**Q: What professional resources do BBA students get?**
Access to industry-recommended tools such as Google Cloud & MS Suite, subscription to Gen-AI and writing tools, Harvard Business Review access, access to a city workstation, social and global immersion, and industry and academic mentors — supporting early professional identity formation. **Q: What do the course-category terms mean?**
University Core: fundamental courses mandated across disciplines. Faculty Core: foundational courses of the faculty/domain. Program Core: mandatory program-specific courses. Professional/Program Electives: specialized advanced electives. Open Electives: flexible electives from other disciplines. Ability Enhancement Courses: communication, reasoning, analytical skills. Skill Enhancement Courses: practical technical/vocational/soft skills. Audit Courses: mandatory non-credit courses that do not affect grades/GPA. Basic Sciences: foundational science courses. Engineering Sciences: fundamental engineering concepts. Research, Project and Internship (RPI): research, project work, internships. Skill Development: industry-aligned professional and employability skills.

**Q: Does the curriculum include internships?**
Yes — every B.Tech programme includes a graded internship ladder under RPI: Social Internship (after Semester 2, 1 credit), Technical Writing (after Semester 4, 1 credit), Summer Internship (after Semester 6, 2 credits), Capstone Project-1/Internship-1 (Semester 7; 5 credits in CSE/AI&ML/DS/CS, 8 credits in SAP), and Capstone Project-2/Internship-2/Research (Semester 8; 10 credits in CSE/AI&ML/DS/CS, 8 credits in SAP). RPI course codes carry the programme prefix: 25CSE (CSE Core), 25AML (AI & ML), 25DSC (Data Science), 25CYS (Cyber Security).

## 3. Eligibility & Entrance Exams

**Q: What is the academic eligibility for B.Tech admission?**
All applicants must have secured a minimum of 60% marks in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) taken together in the 10+2 (Intermediate or equivalent) examination. This academic threshold is mandatory and non-negotiable — it disqualifies any candidate not meeting it, irrespective of entrance examination scores. (Section 4.1 of the policy phrases the same requirement as "60% marks in 10+2 (PCM or aggregate)".)

**Q: Which entrance exams are accepted?**
Admission is based on qualification in any ONE of the following recognized examinations: SUCET (Sreenidhi University Common Entrance Test) — minimum eligibility 60 marks; JEE (Main) — percentile-based; TG EAPCET / AP EAPCET — rank-based. CUET percentile is additionally referenced in the scholarship slabs. There is no composite scoring or weighting system (such as WHMS); selection is strictly based on performance in one accepted entrance exam plus meeting the 10+2 academic cutoff.

**Q: What is the SUCET qualifying score?**
Candidates who score 60 marks or above in SUCET and meet the 60% marks requirement in 10+2 are deemed qualified. Without the SUCET admit card and a valid photo ID, entry to the SUCET exam will be denied.

**Q: Are there extra requirements for NRI/Foreign applicants?**
NRI/Foreign applicants must upload the passport bio-data page, any applicable test scores (e.g., SAT), proof of NRI status of the sponsor (if applying under NRI quota), and an equivalence certificate from AIU (or proof of having applied for one).

### 3.1 SUCET — Eligibility, Criteria & Process

**Q: What is SUCET and who is eligible to take it?**
SUCET (Sreenidhi University Common Entrance Test) is the university's own entrance examination for B.Tech admission. To be eligible, an applicant must have secured a minimum of 60% marks in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) taken together in the 10+2 (Intermediate or equivalent) examination. This 60% academic threshold is mandatory and applies irrespective of the entrance score.

**Q: What is the SUCET qualifying criteria?**
A candidate is deemed qualified by scoring 60 marks or above in SUCET while also meeting the 60% marks requirement in 10+2 (MPC). Selection is based on this single entrance exam plus the academic cutoff — there is no combined or weighted scoring across exams. SUCET is one of three accepted routes for B.Tech admission; the others are JEE (Main) on percentile and TG/AP EAPCET on rank.

**Q: How do I register for SUCET and get my admit card?**
Register and apply online through the official admissions portal — https://apply.suh.edu.in/ — and pay the ₹1,000 application fee; the application is complete only after successful payment. The SUCET admit card (hall ticket) is made available on the portal after the application deadline and once test slots are scheduled. Download and print it — it carries the test centre / online login details and instructions. Without the admit card and a valid photo ID, entry to the SUCET exam is denied.

**Q: What happens after I qualify SUCET?**
There is no centralized counselling for SUCET-based admissions. Candidates who score 60 marks or above and meet the 60% 10+2 (MPC) requirement receive a Provisional Admission Offer Letter through the university online portal. To secure the seat, pay an initial deposit of ₹20,000 (non-refundable), which is adjusted against the first-year total fee; failure to pay within the stipulated deadline results in automatic withdrawal of the offer. Final admission is confirmed after verification of all original documents and payment of the full applicable fees.

**Q: What is the SUCET exam pattern — subjects, duration, and marking?**
The detailed SUCET exam pattern — subjects covered, number of questions, duration, marking scheme, and mode — is shared with candidates along with the official exam notification ahead of the test, and I'll make sure you have the current pattern as part of your application guidance. The key benchmark to keep in mind now is the qualifying score: 60 marks or above, along with 60% in 10+2 (MPC).

## 4. Admission & Application Process

### 4.1 How to Apply

**Q: How do I apply for B.Tech admission at Sreenidhi University?**
Applications must be submitted online through the official University Admissions Portal only — https://apply.suh.edu.in/ (also accessible via the link on www.sreenidhi.edu.in). No offline or third-party application channels are permitted. The University does not authorize any admission agents, and forms submitted via any means other than the official online system will not be accepted.

**Q: What are the steps in the application process?**
The typical steps are:
1. **Registration:** Visit https://apply.suh.edu.in/ and register with a valid email address and mobile number. The email ID provided will be used for all further communication. Upon registration, the applicant receives login credentials (or an activation link) to access the application form.
2. **Form Filling:** Log in and fill the online application form — personal details (name, DOB, parent/guardian info, contact address), academic details (school, Board, year of passing, marks in 10th and 12th), and entrance exam details (SUCET application number, any JEE/EAPCET roll numbers and scores). Candidates also select the program(s) and preferred branch/specialization — preferences can be recorded for use during counselling.
3. **Document Upload:** Upload clear scanned copies of: passport-size photograph (recent, colour) and signature; 10th Standard mark sheet and pass certificate; 10+2 Intermediate mark sheet and pass certificate (or provisional); entrance exam scorecards or rank cards (SUCET, JEE Main/Adv, EAPCET, etc., whichever applicable). NRI/Foreign applicants additionally upload the passport bio-data page, applicable test scores (SAT etc.), proof of NRI status of sponsor (if applying under NRI quota), and an AIU equivalence certificate or proof of having applied for one.
4. **Application Fee Payment:** Pay the non-refundable application fee of ₹1,000 online (credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI). The fee is fixed at ₹1,000 for all Indian applicants irrespective of category. For Foreign/NRI applicants the fee is USD $50 (or INR equivalent as per current exchange rates), payable via international debit/credit card or SWIFT transfer. The application is deemed complete only after successful fee payment — save the payment receipt.
5. **Submission & Acknowledgment:** Review all information and submit electronically. After submission no further changes can be made online (unless a specific edit window is announced). An application ID is generated — note it carefully for all future correspondence and status tracking. Applicants then receive an acknowledgment email with application details and next steps, which may include SUCET admit card download information and the admission process timeline.

**Q: Can I submit more than one application?**
No. Under the Single Application Policy, each candidate should submit only one application. Duplicate applications may lead to cancellation of all applications by that candidate. If corrections are needed after submission, contact the admissions office rather than creating a new application.

**Q: What are the application deadlines?**
The opening and closing dates for application submission are published on the University website and in the official admission notification. Late applications will not be accepted. In exceptional cases (such as proven technical failures on the portal or payment gateway issues on the last day), the University may at its discretion extend the deadline, but candidates are encouraged to apply early and not rely on this.

**Q: How do I get the SUCET admit card?**
For candidates registered for SUCET, the admit card (hall ticket) is made available on the portal after the application deadline and upon scheduling of test slots. Applicants must download and print the admit card, which contains test centre/online login details and instructions. Without the admit card and a valid photo ID, entry to the SUCET exam will be denied.

**Q: How can I track my application status?**
Candidates can track application status (fee paid, documents verified, admit card issued, etc.) by logging into the portal. The University also sends key updates via SMS/email. It is the applicant's responsibility to regularly check email and the portal. Any deficiencies (unreadable documents, missing information) will be communicated for rectification within a given timeframe; failure to correct deficiencies can lead to application rejection.

### 4.2 Counselling, Seat Allotment & Admission Confirmation

**Q: Is there centralized counselling for SUCET admissions?**
No centralized counselling is conducted for SUCET-based admissions. Candidates who score 60 marks or above in SUCET and meet the 60% marks in 10+2 (PCM or aggregate) are deemed qualified, and qualified applicants receive a Provisional Admission Offer Letter via the university online portal.

**Q: How do I secure my seat after receiving the offer?**
To secure the seat, candidates must pay an initial deposit of ₹20,000 (non-refundable), which is adjusted against the first-year total fee. Failure to pay this deposit within the stipulated deadline results in automatic withdrawal of the offer.

**Q: What is the process for EAPCET / JEE (Main) applicants?**
Candidates applying through TG EAPCET / AP EAPCET or JEE (Main) must upload their valid rank card or percentile score through the Sreenidhi University Online Portal. Upon meeting the academic eligibility (minimum 60% in 10+2 MPC) and valid rank/percentile verification, the University issues a Provisional Admission Offer Letter. To confirm the seat, the same initial deposit of ₹20,000 (non-refundable) must be paid within the given timeline.

**Q: Is the provisional admission guaranteed?**
Provisional admission offers are conditional upon verification of eligibility at the time of enrolment. If a candidate fails to produce valid documentation or does not meet the eligibility criteria (either entrance qualification or minimum academic percentage), the provisional admission shall be forfeited. In such cases, the candidate will not be permitted to enrol or continue, and any fee paid may be subject to forfeiture as per university refund policy.

**Q: When is admission finally confirmed?**
Final admission is confirmed only after: verification of all original documents; satisfaction of all academic and regulatory eligibility criteria; and payment of full tuition and applicable fees. The University then issues a Final Admission Confirmation Letter, and candidates may participate in orientation and academic onboarding.

**Q: Is there a management quota or donation route?**
No. The University does not accept any donation or capitation for admission — merit is the sole criteria. Applicants are cautioned not to fall prey to any person/agents who promise admissions in exchange for money or other favours; any such approach should be reported to the University. The application fee is the only fee to be paid at the application stage. If at any stage an admission is found to have been obtained through fraudulent means or misinformation, the admission will be cancelled and appropriate legal action may be taken. The University's decision on all admission-related matters is final and binding.

**Q: Is my personal data safe? Who can I contact for help?**
All personal data collected through the application is kept confidential and used only for admission processing and required statutory reporting; by applying, candidates consent to data use for verification (which might include sharing with exam authorities or boards). For queries or technical difficulties, contact the Admissions Office at admissions@suh.edu.in or the helpline number on the admissions webpage. A dedicated International Admissions Desk is available for foreign applicants (visa, equivalence, etc.). The University's IT support/helpdesk is available to assist with portal difficulties.

### 5.3 Scholarship Continuation / Renewal (From Year-2 Onwards)

**Q: What do I need to do to keep my scholarship after the first year?**
To continue/renew merit scholarships in subsequent years, a student must meet ALL of the following: minimum CGPA 7.0 or above in the regular year exams (first attempt); no backlogs; no disciplinary action; and minimum attendance of 75%.

**Q: Can I combine or switch scholarships?**
Scholarship swapping is not permitted. A student cannot avail multiple scholarships simultaneously, except where a special-category fee waiver is explicitly allowed by the University. If a student is eligible for more than one scholarship, the student may choose the best (highest benefit) scholarship. Students may avail only ONE scholarship/fee waiver in an academic year — if eligible under multiple categories (Merit / Special Category / Sports / NCC / NSS), the student must opt for any one category at the time of fee payment/renewal, and benefits cannot be combined. The policy further states: "If the student opts for the Merit scholarship but does not maintain Merit eligibility in a later year, the student cannot opt for a Special Category or Sports/NCC/NSS scholarship only if independently eligible and after fresh verification/approval for that year." (This condition is worded ambiguously in the official policy — confirm the specifics with admissions.) If a scholarship is discontinued in one year due to non-compliance, the student may apply again in a subsequent year upon meeting the mandatory requirements.
